Before you pull up a chair...

Last month I asked some of you a simple question. Why are you a Christian? Most of us struggled to answer it. Some said because my parents are. Others said because Jesus died for me. Honest answers. None were quite enough.

This evening is different from every gathering we have had so far. We are not going to open one passage and go deep. We are going to open the whole Bible — from Genesis 1 to Revelation 22 — and tell the whole story in one sitting. Four acts. One story. The story of why you exist. The story of why there is a Table in the first place. The story that will give you a one-sentence answer to carry for the rest of your life.

Step 1 — Read
1
Genesis 1:26–27 · Genesis 2:7–9, 15–25

Act One — The Family Table that was.

God speaks in the plural — let us make man in our image. The Father, Son, and Spirit decide to make humans to join the Family they have been enjoying from eternity.

2
Genesis 3:1–24

Act Two — The Table that was lost.

The children walked away from the Table. The door closed. The flaming sword stood at the gate. Every human being since has been born outside the garden.

3
Luke 22:14–20 · Matthew 27:50–51

Act Three — The Table that was reopened.

The body broken. The blood poured out. The temple curtain torn from top to bottom. The door back to the Family Table opened — not by human effort, but by the Son himself.

4
Ephesians 2:4–7

Already seated at the Father's right hand.

The verse most Christians have never been taught clearly. If you have accepted the invitation, you are already seated with Christ in the heavenly places. Not standing. Seated.

5
Revelation 20:4–6 · 21:1–7

Act Four — The Table that will be.

Jesus returns physically to earth. Reigns with his family for a thousand years. Then a new heaven and a new earth, with God himself dwelling with his family forever. The whole story bookended by Family.

Step 2 — The Four Acts
The Whole Bible in One Evening

Four acts. One story.

Every star in every galaxy was arranged as the setting for one thing — a Family Table.

I

Act One · Creation

The Table that was.

The Father, Son, and Spirit — already in perfect love — made humans to share what they had been enjoying from eternity.

II

Act Two · The Fall

The Table that was lost.

The children walked away from the Father in Eden. The door closed. The ache we all feel traces back to this moment.

III

Act Three · Redemption

The Table that was reopened.

The Son came. The body was broken. The blood was poured. The flaming sword fell on him. The door is open again.

IV

Act Four · Restoration

The Table that will be.

Jesus returns. A thousand years of his reign on earth. Then a new heaven and new earth with the Family finally together — forever.

Step 3 — The Sentence to Carry
The One-Sentence Answer

I am a Christian because God is building a Family — and he has invited me to come home.

Not an argument. A story. One sentence to carry for life. One sentence to pass down to your children.
Step 4 — Three Postures at the Table
The Chairs, the Circles, and the Option

Where are you right now?

The invitation is real. But it is still an invitation. Nobody is forced.

Seated at the Table

You have consciously said yes. You have pulled up the chair. The Father is at the head, Jesus is beside you, the Spirit moves among the Family. This is home.

Running in Circles

You see the Table. You know the songs, the stories, the names. But you have never actually sat down. You have been near the Table all your life without being part of the Family.

Considering Leaving

You are seated — but something in you is wondering whether the running-in-circles life might be more interesting. The option to leave is real. But remember — running in circles is also a direction.

Step 5 — What is Coming
The Part Most Teenagers Have Never Been Told

Your life is not seventy years long.

You were not made for a few decades. You were made for forever.

The Return · Revelation 19

Jesus comes back physically.

Not as a baby in a stable. As a King on a white horse. With the armies of heaven behind him. Returning to this earth.

The Rapture · 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17

The Family gathered in one moment.

Every believer across every nation and every century — lifted up at the same moment to meet the Son in the air. The Family finally together.

The Millennium · Revelation 20:4–6

A thousand years of his reign on earth.

Peace. No war. No injustice. No sickness. No evil. Jesus physically present. The Family reigning with him.

The New Earth · Revelation 21:1–4

God himself dwelling with his people forever.

Not us going up to heaven. God coming down to a renewed earth. Eden restored. The Family Table finally completed. Forever.

Step 6 — Every Meal is a Reminder
A New Daily Rhythm

Pause before every meal.

From this evening onwards, before every meal — breakfast, lunch, supper, a snack between meetings — pause for one second. Lift your eyes. And remember. I have a seat at the Family Table. The body was broken for me. The drink was poured out for me. The door was reopened for me. And I am going to pause and thank the Father for inviting me home.

Step 7 — Journal
Question 1

"Before this evening — in your own voice — how would you answer the question why am I a Christian? Be honest. Do not perform."

The honest answer today is the starting point for the deeper answer this evening will give you.
Question 2

"Which of the three postures is closest to where you actually are? Seated at the Table, running in circles, or considering leaving?"

Only you and the Helper know the honest answer. This evening is the night to name it.
Question 3

"What would it look like for you — starting tomorrow morning — to pause before every meal and remember the Family Table?"

Small practice. Lifelong rhythm. Every meal becomes a reminder of what the Table cost.
Step 8 — Come Ready

Bring your Bible

We will walk through Genesis 1 to Revelation 21 in one evening. Keep your Bible open as we move through the four acts.

4–6pm on Sunday

Two hours together. This one may run a little longer because we are telling the whole story. Settle in.

Ages 13 and above

Teenagers, young adults, and parents all welcome. This evening is especially for the young people navigating their own faith.

Come honest

Wherever you are — seated, circling, or considering leaving — bring the honest version of yourself. The Father is waiting at the head of the Table.

A Prayer Before You Come

"Father, I come to this evening with the honest version of myself. Show me where I actually am at your Table. And give me the courage to say yes to the seat you have set for me. Amen."
