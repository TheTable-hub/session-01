Before you pull up a chair...

Last month we discovered that the Shepherd of Psalm 23 became the Host who prepared a table before us in the presence of our enemies. This month we discover something even more extraordinary. The Host who sat across from us at that table is no longer content to sit across from us.

On the night before the cross Jesus said the strangest sentence he ever spoke — "It is for your good that I am going away." Better that he leave? How could that possibly be true? Read these passages slowly this week. What you find will change how you understand your own body.

Step 1 — Read
1
Genesis 1:1–2

Before the world had form.

The very first appearance of the Holy Spirit in scripture — hovering over the waters, bringing form and order out of what was formless and void. His role from the beginning has been to bring order out of chaos.

2
John 14:15–27

The promise of another Helper.

Jesus promises the coming of the Advocate — the Spirit of Truth — who will live with the disciples and be in them forever. Notice the shift from with to in. Everything changes on that preposition.

3
John 16:5–15

Why it is better that he go.

The strangest sentence Jesus ever spoke — and the reason behind it. If he does not leave, the Helper cannot come. If he leaves, the Helper will fill every believer forever.

4
1 Corinthians 3:16 & 6:19

Your body is a temple.

Paul makes it unmistakable. The dwelling place of God is no longer a stone building. It is now every believer who has received the Holy Spirit. You are a Temple. Not one you are building — one you are.

5
Galatians 5:22–23

The fruit of a living Temple.

Nine attributes. One fruit. One Spirit. This is the visible evidence that the Helper has taken up residence in the Temple. What grows on the outside reveals what is at work on the inside.

Step 2 — The Temple on the Move
Where God Has Chosen to Dwell

The presence has been moving all along.

🌊

Stage One · Genesis 1

The Spirit hovering over the waters. Bringing form and shape from formlessness. His first ministry — order out of chaos.

⛺

Stage Two · The Tabernacle and Temple

God's presence dwelt behind a curtain. Approached carefully by one priest, once a year, after elaborate preparation.

✝

Stage Three · Jesus

The Word became flesh. God's presence walking among us in a human body — able to look you in the eye and share your meals.

🪑

Stage Four · The Upper Room

The night before the cross. At the Table, Jesus announced that the presence was about to move again — inward, into every believer.

🔥

Stage Five · Pentecost onward

The Spirit descended. The Temple moved into flesh. Every believer became a living, breathing, walking dwelling place of God.

Step 3 — Meet the Helper
Four Names · One Person

Everything Jesus was to them, the Helper is to you.

The Advocate

He stands in your defence. When accusation whispers against you, he speaks on your behalf — inside you, to the Father.

The Counsellor

The internal wisdom of God, applied to your specific life. Every decision has access to the counsel of heaven.

The Comforter

The one who draws near in your pain. Not distant. Not delayed. Already there — inside the room of your grief.

The Spirit of Truth

He leads you toward what is real, even when what is real is uncomfortable. He surfaces what needs to be surfaced.

Step 4 — The Great Shift
The Table Becomes the Temple

The Helper Within — Six Dimensions

Consider your spinal cord. Hidden. Central. Silent. And without it nothing else works. Six of its features mirror exactly what the Holy Spirit does inside every believer.

1

Firm and Flexible

Not rigid. Not brittle. Firm enough to hold the weight of your convictions — flexible enough to move with every new season of life.

2

Soft and Tender

The intimate presence that delivers spiritual nourishment, clears away waste, and surfaces injury so healing can begin.

3

The Superhighway of Signals

How the will of the Father becomes the movement of your life. Every prayer, every conviction, every step of obedience travels through him.

4

Hidden but Central

He never draws attention to himself. His whole purpose is to glorify Jesus. Yet without him, nothing else in your spiritual life functions.

5

Bringer of Form, Shape and Order

The same Spirit who brought order out of chaos in Genesis 1 is at work in every formless corner of your life — bringing shape where there was none.

6

Wisdom, Knowledge and Understanding

Isaiah 11:2. He carries these through your spiritual nervous system, coordinating every dimension of your life around the person of Jesus Christ.

Step 6 — Journal
Question 1

"Which of the four names of the Helper does your life most need right now — Advocate, Counsellor, Comforter, or Spirit of Truth?"

The Helper is all four at once. But there is likely one your soul is reaching for this week. Which is it?
Question 2

"Which of the nine fruits has been growing most visibly in you over the last year — and which has been hardest to grow?"

Do not be falsely humble. Notice what the Spirit has been doing. Then name honestly where the ground is still hard.
Question 3

"What would this coming week look like if you moved through it as a walking Temple — knowing every room you enter is a room the Spirit has already entered with you?"

This is the question the whole session is building toward. Sit with it before Sunday.
Step 7 — Come Ready

Bring your Bible

We will work through John 14–16, 1 Corinthians 3 and 6, and Galatians 5 together. Any translation you are comfortable with.

4–6pm on Sunday

Two hours together. Come settled and ready to engage. We start and end on time.

Ages 13 and above

Teenagers, young adults, and parents all welcome. Every voice around the table matters.
