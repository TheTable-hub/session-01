Before you pull up a chair...

This session begins with one of the most extraordinary sentences in the entire Bible — that before the world was created, the Father had already released every spiritual blessing for you in Christ. Not some. Every one.

Read the three passages slowly before we gather. Let the question they raise sit with you honestly through the week: if the vault is already open and everything has already been given — what kind of vessel am I presenting to receive it?

Step 1 — Read
1
Ephesians 1:3–14

Every spiritual blessing — already released.

Read slowly and count the blessings Paul lists. Notice how many are described in the past tense — already done, already secured, already given. Let that land before you move on.

2
2 Timothy 2:20–21

Gold, silver, wood and clay.

A large household. Many vessels. All belonging to the same owner. But not all used for the same purposes. Ask yourself honestly — which vessel am I presenting to the Master?

3
Psalm 12:6

Seven times refined.

A single verse that carries more weight than its size suggests. What does it mean that the word of God is like gold refined seven times — and what does that have to do with you?

Step 2 — The Arc
How the Three Passages Connect
💎
Ephesians 1:3–14 — The Treasure

The vault is already open

The Father released every spiritual blessing before the foundation of the world. The question is never his generosity — it is settled. The treasure exists. It has your name on it.

🏺
2 Timothy 2:20–21 — The Vessel

What are you presenting?

The vessel you present determines what you can carry. A clay vessel cannot hold what was designed for gold. The choice of vessel is yours — and it shapes how and what you ask.

🔥
Psalm 12:6 — The Refining

How clay becomes gold

The word of God is seven times refined gold — and immersion in it is the instrument that transforms the vessel. Not willpower. Not performance. The living word doing what only it can do.

Step 3 — The Five Treasures
1

Chosen before creation

Before the world existed — before your name was given — the Father chose you. Not because of performance. According to his pleasure and will.

2

Adopted into sonship

Not servants. Not subjects. Sons and daughters. Predestined for adoption before you drew your first breath.

3

Redemption and forgiveness

Past tense. Secured. Done. The question of your forgiveness was settled at the cross — it does not fluctuate based on your behaviour last Tuesday.

4

The mystery revealed

God did not keep his plan hidden. He made known to us the mystery of his will. He pulled back the curtain and invited us in.

5

Sealed with the Holy Spirit

A deposit guaranteeing the full inheritance. Heaven's legal commitment that everything promised is coming — already placed inside you.

Step 4 — The Vessels
2 Timothy 2:20–21 — Which One Are You?
🥇

Gold

For the most honoured purposes. Cleansed, set apart, fully available to the Master.

🥈

Silver

For honoured use. Refined but not yet fully consecrated to the highest purpose.

🪵

Wood

For everyday common use. Functional but not prepared for special purposes.

🏺

Clay

For common or base use. Shaped by the world rather than the word of God.

Paul is not saying some people are born gold and others born clay with no hope of change. He says those who cleanse themselves will be instruments for special purposes. The choice is active, daily, and entirely yours.

Step 5 — Journal
Question 1

"Which of the five treasures in Ephesians 1 feels most real to you right now — and which one feels furthest away?"

Do not write the answer you think sounds right. Write what is actually true for you today.
Question 2

"What is still clay in you that you are willing to bring to the word of God and allow the refining to begin?"

Be specific. General answers do not produce specific transformation.
Question 3

"The Father's hand is extended. The treasure is already given. What is your response to that invitation?"

This is the question the whole session is building toward. Sit with it honestly before you arrive.
Step 6 — Come Ready

Bring your Bible

We will walk through all three passages together. Ephesians 1, 2 Timothy 2 and Psalm 12.

4–6pm on Sunday

Two hours together. Come settled and ready to engage the word honestly.

Ages 13 and above

Teenagers, young adults, and parents all welcome. Every voice around the table matters.

No pressure

Bring your honest questions. The dance between God's generosity and your response is what this evening is about.

A Prayer Before You Come

"Father, I come not with a bowl begging to be filled — but as a vessel willing to be refined. Show me what is still clay. And do in me what only your word can do. Amen."
